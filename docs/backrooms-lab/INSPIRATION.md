# Backrooms lab: inspiration shortlist

Research date: 14 November 2026. This is an optional design catalogue for Fable, not an implementation specification or a claim that every idea belongs in one run.

## Which Backrooms?

The [Fandom FAQ](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Backrooms_Wiki%3AFAQ) explicitly describes multiple interpretations and says no wiki is the single official source. Its [canon list](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Canon_List) groups community storylines. These are inspiration sources, not a film continuity checklist. [A24's film page](https://a24films.com/films/backrooms) describes Kane Parsons' film and its interconnected-room premise; it does not establish that the creatures below appear in the movie.

Direct Fandom page access was blocked. The research below uses substantial search-indexed article excerpts, which may lag edits. Several entity articles are being rewritten. Verify their current versions before a direct adaptation.

## Rooms: familiar architecture, one impossible detail

All proposed names and interactions in the last column are **original adaptations**, not established wiki lore.

| Source | Source motif | Our room possibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Level 4: Abandoned Office](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Level_4) | Empty offices, furniture impressions, rainy windows, returning stairwells; generally safe. | **Rain Department:** one warm mug amid abandoned desks. The stairwell returns to the same room with one chair relocated. A quiet refuge. |
| [Level 2: Pipe Dreams](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Level_2) | Pipe corridors and uncertain geometry. | **Facilities Detour:** a kitchenette opens onto a long service passage. Stopping a hissing pipe makes a distant fluorescent fixture audible. |
| [Level 37: Sublimity](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Level_37) | Tiled pools, luminous interiors, unusual acoustics; no recorded entity encounters. | **Mandatory Wellness:** office showers lead to enormous pools. One wheeled office chair occupies an island. Keep the encounter peaceful. |
| [Level 5: Terror Hotel](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Level_5) | Hotel rooms, ballrooms, maintenance spaces and distant music or chatter. | **Off-site Conference:** applause comes from the next room. Opening the door reveals the same conference setup, with every chair facing the player. |
| [The End](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/The_End) | Nearly empty library, irregular shelves, vintage computers and unusually muted sound. | **Records Retention:** archive shelves contain no files. A copier produces a wordless image of a nearby doorway. Shelves subtly rearrange behind the player. |
| [Level 108: World of Chains](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Level_108) | Foggy concrete spaces, chain-covered pillars and friendly inhabitants. | **Service Void:** a supply cupboard opens onto a huge shaft. A caretaker lowers a crate bridge after receiving its dropped tool. |

**First recommendation: Carbon Copy.** Develop an original office copy-room encounter around repetition, an ordinary copier, and one spatial discrepancy. It should communicate through objects, sound and player action, without explanatory signage. Carbon Copy is our concept; do not attribute its name or mechanic to the wiki. Keep it separable so Fable can adopt its room, prop or interaction independently.

## Creatures: tension needs harmless encounters too

The suggested office behaviors below are our inventions unless explicitly described as source traits.

| Inspiration | Source traits | Original encounter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Smiler](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_3) — hostile | Luminous face in darkness; this Fandom version is vulnerable to light. | An archive stalker retreats from movable desk lamps. Give pursuit a readable sound and visual warning. |
| [Hound](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_8) — hostile | Canid/humanoid quadruped in derelict spaces. | A low silhouette beneath cubicles, diverted by a rolling can or chair. Sound distraction is our mechanic. |
| [Faceling](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_9) — neutral/helpful | Many adults undertake ordinary activities and mind their own business; behavior varies. | A night porter endlessly mops one tile. Return its bucket and it holds open a staff door. |
| [Male Deathmoth](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_4) — potentially friendly | Smaller, generally passive males can be tamed with Almond Water; females are dangerous. | A desk moth accepts a bottle-cap drink, then guides the player briefly. Do not assume every canon makes moths light-seeking. |
| [Kat](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_17) — companion | Small catlike creatures associated with plants; affectionate but protective of nests. | A blinking clump in a planter eventually follows, then sits facing an overlooked passage. Our voxel version needs a readable scale. |
| [Konelo](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_48) — friendly | Social, curious climber/diver forms associated with Level 108. | The Service Void caretaker trades gestures and tools, then resumes an incomprehensible maintenance task. |

## Items and pacing

- [Almond Water](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Object_1): provisions and trade motif; an unbranded carton could support the moth encounter without adding survival bookkeeping.
- [Level Keys](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Object_2): unusual keys reveal hidden entrances and resonate nearby. Our ceramic key could make faint water sounds near a pool doorway.
- [Dispositive FAX](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Object_45): communication-device inspiration. A fax printing changing room silhouettes would be our supernatural twist.
- Original office objects: rubber doorstop, meeting timer, mug, paper stack and wheeled chair. Prefer one intelligible interaction per object.

Design recommendation: separate rare authored discoveries with quiet repetition and small deviations. An inaccessible room glimpse or recurring chair can imply more space than constant spectacle. Preserve a dependable way to leave the easter egg while making the fictional escape less immediate.

## Original work and source handling

Create original code, voxel models, sounds and scene scripts. Copy no article prose, screenshots, photographs or audio. The wiki footer states CC-BY-SA unless otherwise noted; individual media have separate credits. Retain source URLs, credited authors and access dates, and review article-specific attribution/share-alike requirements before directly adapting distinctive named lore. Renaming a direct adaptation does not remove those considerations.
