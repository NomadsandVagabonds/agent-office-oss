# BlockRooms reference: the owner's own images

The quality bar for raising rooms. All of these are the owner's own: digital art, and renders they made with Codex.
They are here so the agents can match against them. (Found images from other media are never put in this repo.)

What to take from each, in the owner's direction: empty, our own palette, one wrong thing, and the lighting and the
wear doing most of the work. Variety is the game: none of these is to be dealt more often than any other room.

| File | What it is | What to take |
|---|---|---|
| `render-giant-chair.webp` | A long corridor with an ordinary armchair at its end; up close the chair is room-high, its back through the ceiling | the scale reveal: small from far off, huge when you arrive; clipping through the ceiling |
| `render-carpet-mound.webp` | The carpet heaved up into an arched tunnel you can walk through, torn stepped edges, a stapler, dry stalks at the skirting | the building behaving like something alive; stepped voxel edges as a texture of damage |
| `render-in-tray.webp` | A desk letter tray the size of a building, a whole room with a door in each slot, seen from an ordinary desk | "a world of another room visible in each slot"; the small tray on the desk holds tiny rooms too |
| `render-blinds.webp` | A free-standing blind frame on wheels, no window behind it, still throwing sun slats across the room; one paper cup | light from nowhere; one object, a lot of empty |
| `art-burning-room.jpg` | The owner's art: a bedroom open to a dusk sky, a road's yellow line running through it, a fire, a calm figure, the town dissolving into blocks | inside and outside come apart; ordinary things in the wrong place; calm in catastrophe; one hot light against cold dark; the world breaking into blocks |

The owner's hallway-connector renders (the twist, the crossing, the chain, the accordion partitions, the split level,
the fins) were shared in chat only; their notes are in the builder's queue, and the images are to follow.
