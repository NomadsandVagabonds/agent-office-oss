BlockRooms privacy policy

Playing as a guest

Your browser keeps your character, settings and game progress on this device. You can play without giving us an email address. The site counts visits and game starts. It uses a temporary identifier derived from your connection to limit duplicate counts. The homepage can use an approximate city from your connection for its welcome message.

Signing in and saving

If you choose Google sign-in, Google gives BlockRooms your account identifier, your email address and information used to check that address. We use them to sign you in and find your private game archive. Your existing progress on this device is added to that archive, so you can use your character, sightings, scores and recovered tapes on another device.

BlockRooms uses secure cookies for sign-in. Signing out ends the session on this device. A session otherwise expires after 60 days. The game owner can see your sign-in method, account email, and when you signed up, signed in and saved. This helps us count sign-ups and support players.

What other players see

If you sign the guestbook, your nickname, character portrait, message, score and run details, including the building seed, are public. The homepage features the highest-scoring visitor. Your account email and private archive stay out of the guestbook.

Service providers

Vercel hosts the website. Cloudflare processes account requests and stores cloud saves and guestbook entries. Both process connection information to serve the game. Google handles Google sign-in. If you request an email code when that option is available, Resend receives your email address and delivers the code. The code expires in ten minutes.

Your data

To ask about your data or request deletion of your cloud account and archive, email justrmil@gmail.com from the address you used to sign in. Mention any public guestbook entry you also want removed. You can erase the local copy on a device by clearing the browser’s site data for BlockRooms.
