About

Hi, my name is Carl. I work in Facilities & Toner on Sub-Level B2. Management says the floors below B2 are not on the building lease, so I made this page on my lunch break to keep track of them.

Nov '98 update: Everything below my cardigan is in the drywall outside Room 14 now, so I have plenty of time to look after the page and the Webring. Take a camcorder and spare batteries — the rooms are not in the same place twice.
